Selling Prized Vintage Motorcycle

The Restoration

Motorcycles have been an important part of my life for many years. One of my most prized possessions is a vintage motorcycle that I restored with help from friends and members of the veteran community. What started as an old motorcycle became a project that required a lot of time, patience, and teamwork.

Restoring the motorcycle has been more than getting it back on the road. Working on it gave me an opportunity to spend time with other veterans who shared an interest in motorcycles. Because of those experiences, the motorcycle has personal value that goes far beyond what it is worth financially.

Why I'm selling

I am currently attending Penn State and working toward my degree. College tuition is expensive, so I have made the difficult decision to sell my motorcycle to help cover the cost. I would rather keep something that has so many memories attached to it, but completing my education is more important.

Why This Motorcycle is Special

  • It was restored with help from friends and members of the Veteran community.
  • The restoration required a lot of time, patience, and teamwork.
  • It represents years of memories and my passion for motorcycles.
