Restored Vintage Motorcycle

About the Motorcycle

This vintage motorcycle was restored with help from friends and members of the veteran community. The restoration required many hours of work, patience, and attention to detail to get the motorcycle back on the road.

The motorcycle combines its vintage appearance with several restored components that make it ready to ride. Care was taken during the restoration to maintain the classic look of the motorcycle.

Motorcycle Details

  • Classic military green paint
  • Restored engine and exhaust system
  • Updated brakes and electrical components
  • Original-style seat and handlebars
  • Restoration and maintenance records included
