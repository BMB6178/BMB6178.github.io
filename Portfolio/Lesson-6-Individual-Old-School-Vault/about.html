About The Old School Vault

Welcome to The Old School Vault, where we belive great music never goes ot of style. Our record store celebrates the artists and albums that helped define generations of music.

Before streaming services and digital playlists, music lovers experienced their favorite artists through vinyl records. From carefully examining album covers to placing the needle on a record, listening to music was an experience.

At The Old School Vault, we celebrate classic rock legends including Pink Floyd, Fleetwood Mac, Led Zepplin, the Eagles and AC/DC.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: Keeping the 1900s Spinning in 2026 and Beyond.

We want to introduce new generations to the music that shaped the past while giving longtime vinyl collectors a place to rediscover their favorite albums.
